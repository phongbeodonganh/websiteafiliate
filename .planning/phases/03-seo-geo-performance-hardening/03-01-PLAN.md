<objective>
Replace the read-modify-write view counting anti-pattern on the article page with a fire-and-forget after() + atomic $inc with IP+article dedupe, and layer React.cache() + unstable_cache() for cross-request article data caching.

Purpose: The article page currently writes to the database on every render (article.save()), causing lost-update races, F5 count inflation, and an extra round-trip on every view. Moving to after() + $inc + dedupe fixes all three. Layering unstable_cache means crawler/ad-traffic bursts serve from cache instead of hammering Atlas.

Output: src/lib/view-count.ts (new), src/app/article/[slug]/page.tsx (modified), src/lib/cache-revalidation.ts (modified), 2 test files.
</objective>
